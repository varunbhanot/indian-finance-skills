# Primary-source facts for FY 2026-27 (checked 2026-09-06)

Legend: PRIMARY = government / regulator / statute text read directly. LEAD = primary document identified but not read directly here (site blocked from this sandbox); text confirmed from a verbatim copy hosted elsewhere, as noted.

## 1. GST on individual life insurance premiums — EXEMPT from 22 Sep 2025

**Rule now:** Nil GST on "Services of life insurance business provided by an insurer to the insured, where the insured is not a group" (new Sl. No. 36C, Heading 9971, inserted in the services-exemption notification 12/2017-Central Tax (Rate)). Explanation in the entry: exemption applies to "a contract of insurance where the insured is an individual, or an individual and family of the said individual". Sl. No. 36D does the same for health insurance; Sl. No. 36E exempts reinsurance of 36C/36D. Para 2, new clause (zfb) defines "group" (employer–employee groups and non-employer–employee groups with a relationship other than insurance) — so group term / group credit life stay at 18%. Clause 2: "This notification shall come into force with effect from the 22nd day of September, 2025."

- Notification No. **16/2025-Central Tax (Rate)**, dated 17 September 2025, **G.S.R. 666(E)**, F. No. 190341/188/2025-TRU, Gazette of India Extraordinary Part II Sec. 3(i). Parallel: 16/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate) (G.S.R. 667(E)) and 16/2025-Union Territory Tax (Rate), same date.
  - Title as it appears on CBIC: "Seeks to amend Notification No 12/2017-Central Tax (Rate) dated 28.06.2017 to implement the recommendations of the 56th GST Council".
  - PRIMARY URL (listing page; PDF link is on it): https://cbic-gst.gov.in/central-tax-rate.html — the CBIC hosts return 403/404/503 to this sandbox, so the gazette text above was read from a verbatim copy of the gazette pages at https://a2ztaxcorp.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/GST-Notifications-pertaining-to-Services-CGST-IGST-UTGST-N.-No.-16-2025.pdf (secondary host, primary text). Cite the CBIC URL; do not cite a2ztaxcorp.
- **Coverage of ULIP / endowment / money-back / term:** the notification itself does not list product types — it exempts all "services of life insurance business" to non-group insureds. Product coverage is stated by the Government's own FAQ: PIB, Ministry of Finance, "Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on the decisions of the 56th GST Council held in New Delhi", 3 Sep 2025, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163560 (PRIMARY, read): "The policies covered under the exemption recommended on life insurance are all individual life insurance policies including term, ULIP, and endowment plans and reinsurance services thereof." Money-back plans are endowment-type non-linked plans and fall inside "all individual life insurance policies"; there is no separate carve-out. Also PIB "Frequently Asked Questions-2 (FAQs-2) on the decisions of the 56th GST Council", 16 Sep 2025, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2167151 (PRIMARY, read) Q4–Q5: exemption covers individual, or individual-with-family, contracts; reinsurance also exempt; insurers must reverse ITC on other inputs.
- 56th GST Council recommendation itself: PIB, "Recommendations of the 56th Meeting of the GST Council held at New Delhi", 3 Sep 2025, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163229 (mirror PDF read at https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/press_release_press_information_bureau_0.pdf): "Exemption of GST on all individual life insurance policies whether term life, ULIP or endowment policies and reinsurance thereof".
- **Single premium:** not treated separately — a single-premium individual policy is an individual life-insurance service and is exempt; GST applies by date of premium payment (time-of-supply), so a premium paid on/after 22 Sep 2025 is exempt; premiums paid earlier are not refunded. That timing statement comes from the Department of Financial Services page "Exemption of GST on all Individual Life Insurance and Health Insurance Policies", https://www.financialservices.gov.in/exemption-gst-all-individual-life-insurance-and-health-insurance-policies and its FAQ PDF https://www.financialservices.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/Insurance-FAQs.pdf (LEAD — DFS site returned 503 to this sandbox; content seen only via search snippets).
- **Before the change (what the 2024 PRD encoded):** rate was 18% on a *value of supply* fixed by **Rule 32(4), CGST Rules, 2017**: (a) gross premium less the amount allocated to investment/savings *if that amount is intimated to the policyholder* (this is the ULIP case — 18% on charges only); (b) single-premium annuity policies: 10% of the single premium (→ 1.8% effective); (c) all other cases: 25% of premium in the first year and 12.5% in subsequent years (→ 4.5% / 2.25% effective — the PRD's figures); proviso: rule does not apply where the entire premium is for risk cover (pure term → 18% on the whole premium). PRIMARY: Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017, Rule 32(4), https://cbic-gst.gov.in/ (CGST Rules PDF under "GST Law"); text confirmed via https://indiankanoon.org/doc/36673163/. Rule 32(4) is still on the books; it is simply irrelevant to exempt individual policies and still governs group policies.
- **Change date:** 22 Sep 2025 (before: 18% on Rule 32(4) value; after: Nil for individual/family contracts).

## 2. Income-tax exemption of life insurance proceeds — Income-tax Act, 2025

**Provision:** **Schedule II (see section 11), Table, Sl. No. 2** — "Any sum received under a life insurance policy, including the sum allocated by way of bonus on such policy." The old s.10(10D) of the 1961 Act.

- Act: **The Income-tax Act, 2025 (No. 30 of 2025)**, assented 21 Aug 2025, in force 1 Apr 2026 (s.1(3)). Gazette of India Extraordinary Part II Sec. 1, No. 35, 21 Aug 2025.
  - PRIMARY URL: https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf (egazette fails TLS from this sandbox; the gazette text was read from ICAI's verbatim copy https://resource.cdn.icai.org/87647dtc-aps2139-inceome-tax-act-2025.pdf, 572 pp., same gazette pagination). Section-wise on the department site: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/schedule-ii-9 (403 to this sandbox) and the consolidated "Income-tax Act, 2025 [30 of 2025] as amended by Finance Act, 2026": https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf (403 here — **not checked for Finance Act 2026 amendments; verify before pinning**).
- **Conditions (column C), except where the sum is received on death:** by policy issue date —
  1. 1 Apr 2003–31 Mar 2012: premium-to-sum-assured ratio ≤ 20%.
  2. 1 Apr 2012–31 Mar 2013: ≤ 10%.
  3. 1 Apr 2013–31 Jan 2021: ≤ 15% for a "special policy", ≤ 10% otherwise.
  4. 1 Feb 2021–31 Mar 2023: ULIP — ratio test **and** "aggregate of premium for all such policies (in any of the tax years during the term of all of such policies) is ≤ ₹ 2,50,000"; non-ULIP — ratio test only.
  5. On or after 1 Apr 2023: ULIP — ratio test and aggregate ≤ ₹ 2,50,000; **other than ULIP — ratio test and aggregate ≤ ₹ 5,00,000**.
  - (b) the ₹2.5L/₹5L aggregate conditions do not apply to policies issued on/after 1 Apr 2025 by an IFSC Insurance Office; (c) sums under s.127(4) and Keyman policies are never excluded.
  - Note 1(d): "premium to sum assured ratio" = "the highest percentage of annual premium payable to the actual capital sum assured, during the term of the policy". Note 1(a): "actual capital sum assured" per **Schedule XV para 2(2)**: "the minimum amount assured under the policy on happening of the insured event at any time during the term of the policy, not taking into account (a) the value of any premiums agreed to be returned; or (b) any benefit by way of bonus or otherwise over and above the sum actually assured". "Special policy" = policy on a person with disability (s.154) or specified disease (rules under s.128).
- Nothing changed in substance versus 1961 s.10(10D) as amended by FA 2021 and FA 2023; only the citation changed on 1 Apr 2026.

## 3. PPF interest rate, FY 2026-27, and the ₹1,50,000 ceiling

- **Rate: 7.1% p.a.** for Q1 (1 Apr–30 Jun 2026) and Q2 (1 Jul–30 Sep 2026). Unchanged since the Jan–Mar 2024 quarter revision.
- Q1 instrument: Ministry of Finance, Department of Economic Affairs (Budget Division), Office Memorandum **F.No.1/4/2019-NS dated 30.03.2026**, "Revision of interest rates for Small Savings Schemes – reg.": rates for Q1 FY 2026-27 "shall remain unchanged from those notified for the fourth quarter (1st January, 2026 to 31st March, 2026) of FY 2025-26".
- Q2 instrument: same file number, **OM dated 30.06.2026**: Q2 rates "shall remain unchanged from those notified for the first quarter (1st April, 2026 to 30th June, 2026) of FY 2026-27".
- PRIMARY URL for both: DEA "Small Savings" page https://dea.gov.in/budget-division/475 (each quarter's OM PDF is linked there; dea.gov.in dropped the connection from this sandbox). Both OMs were confirmed verbatim from Department of Posts SB Order No. 01/2026 (30.03.2026) and SB Order No. 07/2026 (30.06.2026), which enclose them (copies read at staffnews.in — secondary host). Indian Bank's small-savings page also quotes "OM F.No.1/4/2019-NS dated 30.06.2026".
- Caveat: the OMs only say "unchanged"; the 7.1% figure itself chains back through the same-numbered OMs to the Q4 FY 2023-24 OM (dated 29.12.2023) that last set PPF at 7.1%. A rules file should cite the current-quarter OM plus that base OM.
- **Oct–Dec 2026:** no OM exists yet; the Q3 OM is normally issued on/about 30 Sep 2026.
- **₹1,50,000 ceiling:** **Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019**, notified by G.S.R. 915(E) dated 12 Dec 2019 (Gazette Part II Sec. 3(i)), **paragraph 4 "Limits of subscription"**: "(1) A deposit which shall not be less than five hundred rupees and not more than one lakh fifty thousand rupees in multiple of fifty rupees may be made in an account in a year. (2) Maximum limit of one lakh fifty thousand rupees … shall be inclusive of the deposits made in his own account and in the account opened on behalf of the minor." PRIMARY: gazette copy hosted by India Post, https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/PublicProvidentFundScheme2019English.pdf (read). Also on NSI: https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=169.

## 4. IRDAI surrender value rules (non-linked) and Insurance Act s.113

- **Regulation:** IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024, F. No. IRDAI/Reg/8/202/2024, Hyderabad 20 Mar 2024, Gazette of India Extraordinary Part III Sec. 4, No. 206, 22 Mar 2024; in force from gazette date or 1 Apr 2024 whichever later. Repeals IRDAI (Acquisition of Surrender and Paid up Values) Regulations, 2015. PRIMARY (read): https://irdai.gov.in/web/guest/document-detail?documentId=4590475 (PDF link on that page).
  - **Schedule I, clause 4(A)(a) — GSV, other than single premium** (policy acquires GSV "on payment of premium for at least two consecutive years"), at least: 30% of total premiums paid (less survival benefits paid) if surrendered in year 2; 35% in year 3; 50% in years 4–7; 90% in the last two policy years, with smooth progression beyond year 7 converging to ≥ 90%. **Single premium:** 75% within year 3; 90% in year 4; 90% in last two years with smooth progression beyond year 4. 4(A)(a)(3): surrender value of subsisting bonus / guaranteed additions is added. 4(A)(a)(5): surrender value = higher of GSV and SSV. 4(A)(a)(4): SSV represents the asset share (par) or notional asset share / guaranteed benefits (non-par).
  - **Paid-up value, 4(A)(a)(7):** paid-up sum assured on death (or maturity) ≥ (period for which premiums were paid ÷ maximum period for which premiums were originally payable) × sum assured on death (or maturity); (6): a policy that has acquired surrender value does not lapse but continues as paid-up; (8): does not apply if paid-up SA < ₹2,500 (₹500 micro-insurance) or annuity < ₹250/month.
- **Master Circular:** "Master Circular on Life Insurance Products", Ref. IRDAI/ACTL/MSTCIR/MISC/89/6/2024, 12 Jun 2024 (69 pp.). PRIMARY (read): https://irdai.gov.in/document-detail?documentId=380461 (English PDF linked there; direct link is the long irdai.gov.in/documents/37343/... URL). Clause 26.4: SSV "shall be at least equal to the expected present value of (a) paid-up sum assured on all contingencies covered and (b) paid-up future benefits (such as income benefits), if any, and (c) accrued/vested benefits, duly allowing for survival benefits already paid"; 26.4.2: SSV "shall become payable after completion of first policy year provided one full year premium has been received" (immediately after first premium for limited-pay < 5 years and single premium); 26.4.3.2: discount rate "shall not be more than the prevailing yield on 10 Year G-Sec with a spread of not exceeding 50 basis points"; 26.4.4: SSV reviewed annually; 26.5: policy-year-wise GSV, SSV and surrender value to be shown in the benefit illustration. Existing products had to be modified with effect from **1 Oct 2024** (clauses on implementation: old products could continue up to 30 Sep 2024).
  - No amendment to this Master Circular found as of Sep 2026 (searches surfaced none; it says it is reviewed annually — check the IRDAI circulars list before pinning).
- **Insurance Act, 1938, s.113** (as substituted by the Insurance Laws (Amendment) Act, 2015): "(1) A policy of life insurance shall acquire surrender value as per the norms specified by the regulations. (2) Every policy of life insurance shall contain the formula as approved by the Authority for calculation of guaranteed surrender value of the policy. (3) … a policy which has acquired a surrender value shall not lapse by reason of non-payment of further premiums but shall be kept in force to the extent of paid-up sum insured, calculated by means of a formula as approved by the Authority and contained in the policy and the reversionary bonuses … (4) does not apply where paid-up sum insured is below the Authority-specified amount, or where the parties agree otherwise in writing after default." **The pro-rata formula is no longer in s.113 itself** (it was in the pre-2015 s.113(2)); today it lives in Schedule I cl. 4(A)(a)(7) of the 2024 Regulations above. PRIMARY: India Code, https://www.indiacode.nic.in/show-data?actid=AC_CEN_2_33_00044_193804_1523351752525&orderno=241 (LEAD — India Code returned 403/404 here; text read at https://indiankanoon.org/doc/150449/).

## 5. Benefit-illustration return scenarios (4% and 8%)

- Same Master Circular on Life Insurance Products (IRDAI/ACTL/MSTCIR/MISC/89/6/2024, 12 Jun 2024), PRIMARY, read:
  - **Non-linked (Part B), clause 33.1:** "Except for products where all the benefits are assured in absolute amount at the outset of the contract, all other insurance products, in the customised benefit illustration, shall illustrate the guaranteed and non-guaranteed benefits at gross investment returns. Considering the dynamic nature of movement of interest rates, such gross investment returns are currently stipulated as 4% p.a. and 8% p.a, for other than annuity products." → applies to **participating non-linked plans** (and any non-linked product with non-guaranteed benefits); fully guaranteed non-par plans are exempt from the two-scenario illustration.
  - **Linked (Part C), clause 54.7.1:** "currently the gross investment returns are stipulated as 4% p.a. and 8% p.a. The corresponding net yield shall be demonstrated only with respect to gross investment return as stipulated by the Competent Authority. Currently such rate is 8% p.a." Clause 7.15.1: illustrated ULIP fund value at maturity must be ≥ 90% of total premiums at 4% gross yield.
  - Chapter 1 cl. 3 and Regulations Sch. I cl. 4(6)(ii): the customised illustration must be signed by the prospect and the seller, incorporate surrender values, and form part of the policy document.
- Caveat: the circular says "currently stipulated", i.e. the 4%/8% pair is set by the Competent Authority and can change by circular; nothing newer found as of Sep 2026.

## 6. LTCG on listed equity / equity MF, FY 2026-27 — Income-tax Act, 2025, **section 198**

- **Rate 12.5% on LTCG exceeding ₹1,25,000** (s.198(2)(a): "income-tax calculated on such long-term capital gains exceeding ₹ 125000 at the rate of 12.5%"); the balance of income taxed as if the LTCG were not there (s.198(2)(b)).
- **Assets (s.198(1)(b)):** long-term capital asset being an equity share in a company, a unit of an equity oriented fund, or a unit of a business trust.
- **STT condition (s.198(1)(c)):** STT under Chapter VII of Finance (No. 2) Act, 2004 paid "on acquisition and transfer" for equity shares; "on transfer" for units of an equity-oriented fund or business trust. s.198(4): STT condition waived for IFSC exchange trades settled in foreign currency; s.198(5): Government may notify acquisitions exempt from the acquisition-STT condition (successor to the 2017 notification under s.112A).
- s.198(3): resident individual/HUF may set the unused basic exemption limit against the LTCG. s.198(6): Chapter VIII deductions not allowed against this LTCG; s.198(7): s.156 rebate computed excluding tax on it. s.198(8): "equity oriented fund" definition (includes ULIP funds to which Schedule II Sl. No. 2 exemption does not apply — i.e. ULIPs over the ₹2.5L aggregate are taxed as equity funds).
- Corresponds to s.112A of the 1961 Act (which had been 12.5% / ₹1,25,000 since 23 Jul 2024 under Finance (No. 2) Act 2024; 10% / ₹1,00,000 before that).
- PRIMARY: same gazette PDF as item 2, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265620.pdf (gazette pp. 222–223); department page https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-198 (403 here). Surcharge on this LTCG is capped at 15% for individuals — that lives in the Finance Act 2026 rate schedule, not in s.198; not verified here.
- Caveat: check the "as amended by Finance Act, 2026" consolidation before pinning — I could not open it.

## 7. Official inflation / equity-return benchmarks

- **Inflation — yes, a statutory one.** RBI Act, 1934, **s.45ZA**: Central Government, in consultation with RBI, notifies the CPI inflation target once every five years. Current notification: Department of Economic Affairs gazette notification of **25 Mar 2026** — target **4%**, upper tolerance **6%**, lower tolerance **2%**, for **1 Apr 2026–31 Mar 2031** (previous: 31 Mar 2021 notification for 1 Apr 2021–31 Mar 2026, same numbers; original 5 Aug 2016 for 5 Aug 2016–31 Mar 2021). PRIMARY, read: RBI, "Monetary Policy — Overview" (Function-wise site), https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_Overview.aspx?fn=2752, which states: "In its second review, on March 25, 2026, the Central Government retained the inflation target and the tolerance band for the next 5-year period – April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2031." The gazette notification itself (S.O. number) is on egazette.gov.in / dea.gov.in — LEAD only; neither site was reachable from here and no secondary source quoted the S.O. number. The 2015 Monetary Policy Framework Agreement (20 Feb 2015) is historical background — PIB release https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?relid=124605 — and was superseded by the 2016 statutory framework; cite s.45ZA and the 2026 notification, not the Agreement.
  - A target is a policy objective, not a forecast; a tool may cite it only as "the statutory target", not as an expected inflation rate.
- **Equity return — no regulator publishes one.** IRDAI's 4%/8% are illustration conventions, not return benchmarks (item 5). SEBI/AMFI publish no official expected-return figure. The closest citable primary document for realised history is NSE Indices' own factsheet: "Nifty 50 Index" factsheet dated **31 Aug 2026**, https://www.niftyindices.com/Factsheet/ind_nifty50.pdf (PRIMARY, read): Total Return Index since-inception CAGR **12.38%** (Price Return 10.86%; 5-year TR 8.32%; base date 3 Nov 1995, base value 1000). NSE Indices also publishes the "Methodology Document for Equity Indices" (https://www.niftyindices.com/Methodology/Method_NIFTY_Equity_Indices.pdf) defining the TR index; downloadable daily TRI history is at niftyindices.com under Reports → Historical Data. Caveats: the factsheet URL is overwritten monthly (no stable per-month URL — archive the PDF and record the "as of" date), its disclaimer says the data is "for reference purpose only", and a since-inception CAGR is a fact about the past, not a return assumption; under ADR 0005/0007 the tool should not turn it into a projection.
