# A twelve-month cliff moves nothing between the years

`cliff_months: 12` is carried onto the grant's rows in `year_by_year.grants`,
and every one of the four years is ₹10,00,000 — exactly what the same schedule
without a cliff would give. That is the fixture's point: a cliff is a condition
on whether the first vest happens at all, not a rescheduling of it, and the
decoder does not redistribute a rupee on account of one.

The cliff is carried as the months the letter states, and not as a sentence.
What it means for a reader who leaves inside the first year is the skill's to
say (CLAUDE.md's two-layer rule); the core states the fact and the rows it does
not change.
