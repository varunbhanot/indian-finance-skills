# A back-loaded 5/15/40/40 grant, year one against the average

The reading the year-by-year table exists for. The letter quotes ₹80,00,000 of
restricted stock units; 2,000 units at ₹3,800 values them at ₹76,00,000, and the
schedule delivers 5% of that in year one and 80% in years three and four.

So the first row totals ₹23,80,000 against an average of ₹39,00,000 — the same
package, and the same four years, read two ways. The decoder puts the two
figures side by side and computes no difference between them (ADR 0007).

Both equity columns are here for the same reason they are everywhere else: the
valued vest is what `total` counts, and the letter's own claim for that vest is
carried beside it, unchanged (ADR 0016).
