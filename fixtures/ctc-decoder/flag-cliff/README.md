# flag-cliff

A grant with a twelve-month cliff, so `cliff` is raised. Like `clawback`, its
`kind` is **letter**: the cliff is on the user's own offer letter, not in a
statute and not behind a threshold.

The cliff moves no value between years — the schedule is an even 25% a year and
stays that way. It is raised because a reader asking why year one looks the way
it does needs the answer beside the table.

`equity-perquisite` is raised as it is for any grant. The even schedule keeps
`back-loaded` quiet and the listed grant keeps `unvaluable-share` quiet.
