# flag-one-time-share

A joining bonus at a fifth of year one, above the authored threshold of 15%, so
`one-time-share` is raised.

`₹4,00,000 ÷ ₹19,00,000 = 21.05%`, where year one is guaranteed recurring cash
(basic ₹10,00,000 plus special allowance ₹5,00,000) plus the bonus.

**What the share is measured against matters.** It reads the year-by-year
table's own year-one figures, not `totals.one_time_components`. That total counts
every non-recurring component and an equity grant is one — but a grant does not
land and stop, it spreads over its schedule, so counting it here would raise a
"year two is a pay cut" flag about something that is not a year-two cut. The
bonus carries no clawback period, which keeps `clawback` out of this fixture.
