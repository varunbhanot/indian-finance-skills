# flag-unvaluable-share

An unlisted option grant at a fifth of the claimed package, above the authored
threshold of 10%, so `unvaluable-share` is raised.

`₹10,00,000 ÷ ₹50,00,000 = 20%`.

**Both figures are on the claimed basis** (ADR 0016). The question the flag
answers is how much of what the letter *counted* rests on a figure nobody can
check — so measuring the decoder's own valuation would be measuring nil against
the headline and always reporting nothing.

`equity-perquisite` is raised alongside, as it is for any grant. The schedule is
even, so `back-loaded` does not fire, and there is no cliff.
