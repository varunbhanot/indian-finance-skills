# Listed RSUs, valued at grant-date fair market value

The one case where the decoder puts a number on equity. 800 units at ₹4,500 is
₹36,00,000, against the ₹40,00,000 the letter claims; the gap is the fixture's
point, and the decoder states both rather than the difference.

`method` is `grant-date-fair-market-value` and the assumption says the price is
held flat. Nothing here models growth, and the four vesting years are echoed as
typed rather than divided into.
