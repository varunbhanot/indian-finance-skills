# flag-non-cash-share

Retirals plus benefits in kind at a quarter of CTC, above the authored threshold
of 20%, so `non-cash-share` is raised.

`(₹3,00,000 + ₹2,00,000) ÷ ₹20,00,000 = 25%` — employer provident fund and the
group medical premium against the headline.

Both halves are carried separately in `measured`, because they are different
kinds of not-cash: one is savings the employee owns and cannot reach, the other
is cover they never receive as money at all. Basic is 50% of fixed pay, inside
the band, so `basic-share` stays quiet.
