# A five-year schedule lengthens the table rather than being folded into four

Four years is the shortest table the decoder draws, because four is the horizon
an offer is habitually averaged over (CONTEXT.md, "Back-loaded"). It is a floor
and not a window: this grant vests over five, so the table runs to five on both
bases and the average is over five.

Folding the fifth year into the fourth, or dropping it, would be the decoder
rewriting a schedule the letter states — the same move ADR 0016 refuses when it
requires a schedule to account for the whole grant.
